To play the original studio recording of "Sa Aking Puso" by Kaye Cal:
1. Save your MP3 file as "sa-aking-puso.mp3" (or "wedding-song.mp3") in this folder: assets/audio/
2. When guests tap the music button or open the envelope, your audio track will play automatically.
3. If no file is added, the built-in romantic acoustic synthesizer will play automatically as a fallback.
