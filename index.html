Wedding Dress Code

Theme: Burgundy, Champagne Gold, and Elegant Neutral Tones.

Burgundy

Dusty Rose

Principal Sponsors

Ninongs: Formal Barong Tagalog or Suit with dark trousers.

Ninangs: Long Burgundy or Champagne Gold formal evening gown.

Female Guests

Formal or semi-formal dress in Burgundy, Wine, Champagne Gold, or Beige.

Note: White and cream dresses are reserved for the bride.

Male Guests

Formal Barong Tagalog or tailored suit and tie with dark trousers.

Burgundy or champagne accents are welcome.

"Your presence is our greatest gift. We kindly encourage our guests to wear our suggested wedding colors."
