A Love Written in the Stars

Our Love Story

Every love story is beautiful, but ours is our absolute favorite. Here are the unforgettable milestones that brought us to forever.

October 14, 2018

How We Met

It began unexpectedly on a quiet, rainy Sunday afternoon at a quaint neighborhood bookstore café. A shared laughter over an accidental table mix-up sparked a conversation that turned hours into minutes. We both left knowing that something truly extraordinary had begun.

November 03, 2018

Our First Date

Our first official date took us to the historic streets of Old Manila, followed by a romantic seaside dinner as the sun painted the sky in shades of warm gold and deep burgundy. Walking hand in hand under the city lights, we realized our souls were meant to journey together.

February 14, 2025

The Proposal

At sunset overlooking the rolling hills of Tagaytay, surrounded by hundreds of deep red burgundy roses, fairy lights, and our favorite melody playing softly, Alexander bent on one knee and asked the question that would change our lives forever. Through tears of joy, Isabella said "Yes!"

December 19, 2026

Our Journey Together

Eight years of shared dreams, laughter, overcoming life's adventures, and endless love have led us to this sacred moment. We cannot wait to stand before God, our families, and dearest friends to promise forever.
